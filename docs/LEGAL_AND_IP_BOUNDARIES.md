# Legal and IP Boundaries

Official content must be original and legally independent. Do not bundle, reproduce, host, link to, name, identify, or deliberately imitate third-party franchise characters, creatures, sprites, audio, logos, dialogue, proprietary terminology, or extracted assets. Generic genre concepts and neutral domain language are used throughout code, samples, tests, schemas, and filenames.

The generic data-and-asset architecture may support user-created total conversions, but the official repository does not endorse or distribute unauthorized packs. Pack metadata records authorship, source/provenance, and intended licenses; claims require evidence and do not transfer rights automatically.

Concept mockups are design references only and remain outside the Godot import root. They are not shipped unless explicitly approved with verified rights. Generated source and transformations retain provenance records.

No repository `LICENSE` is created until the product owner selects code and asset/content licenses. Dependencies and tools must later be reviewed for license compatibility before distribution.
