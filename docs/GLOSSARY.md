# Glossary

- **Active partner:** the one pet currently receiving active care and presentation.
- **Care mistake:** a recorded consequence of an important ignored attention call; not every low meter tick.
- **Content API:** versioned contract for packs and their cross-references.
- **Form:** a specific lifecycle/evolution representation of a species/family.
- **Habitat:** the active pet's layered personal environment.
- **Minimal / Small / Expanded:** three presentations of one simulation.
- **Namespaced ID:** stable identifier in `namespace:name` form, independent of visible text.
- **Pack:** versioned JSON and safe-media content unit; the bundled game is also a pack.
- **Resident:** an eligible mature pet safely settled at the farm/sanctuary and recallable.
- **Stable pet instance ID:** identity for one raised pet, distinct from its content form ID.
- **Trading Post:** rotating-order economy loop; not necessarily a simulated restaurant.
