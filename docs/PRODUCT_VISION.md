# Product Vision

KoalaPet begins intimate and becomes expansive. The first screen is not a management dashboard: the player chooses one data-defined egg, hatches one companion, learns its rhythms, and forms a history through care. New systems appear because that relationship created a reason for them.

The everyday product is a small desktop presence. Minimal Mode leaves only the pet and brief signals visible. Small Mode provides the default compact habitat and quick care. Expanded Mode opens detailed history, inventory, evolution, adventure, customization, and later farm management without taking permanent ownership of the desktop.

The classic V-pet foundation includes meaningful needs, attention, waste, sleep, treatment, training, health, age, battles, and branching evolution. KoalaPet extends this with experience, dungeons, unlockable habitat pieces, and eventually a collection of individually remembered pets. Mature companions may settle safely, work passively, and return as the active favorite at any time.

The long loop connects systems: care shapes evolution; battles and dungeons shape history; dungeon themes unlock habitat art, objects, recipes, trophies, materials, eggs, and evolution flags; residents produce resources; Trading Post orders create choices. A full restaurant is optional later scope.

All official content is original and loaded as a mod-compatible pack. The engine remains neutral, local-first, privacy-first, and independent from visible branding.
