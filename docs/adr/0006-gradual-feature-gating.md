# ADR 0006: Gradual Feature Gating

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-08-11

## Context

Showing farm, collection, and economy dashboards at startup weakens the initial bond and overwhelms onboarding.

## Decision

Start with a coherent single-pet V-pet. Adventure, customization, second egg, farm, idle work, and Trading Post appear through data-driven gates when they have purpose. Large unrevealed systems are absent from normal UI.

## Consequences

Unlock facts, grants, migrations, and hidden/hinted presentation require explicit contracts. Roadmap sequencing does not delete accepted later scope.

## Rejected alternatives

All systems visible but locked: clutter and premature management framing. Farm-first: undermines personal raising.

## Revisit triggers

Onboarding evidence may tune gate timing, not the classic-first pillar without product-owner decision.
