# ADR 0007: One Active Partner and Recallable Residents

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-08-11

## Context

Collection growth must preserve attachment without multiplying active-care chores.

## Decision

Maintain one active partner. Mature settled-ready pets can become identity-preserving, safe farm residents, work passively, and be recalled at any time.

## Consequences

Active/resident transitions are atomic. Residents retain full history and avoid repetitive needs/offline-neglect death. Eligibility prevents newborn care bypass.

## Rejected alternatives

Permanent retirement/disposal: breaks favorite retention. Simultaneous care for all: unbounded chores. Frozen newborn storage: bypasses lifecycle intent.

## Revisit triggers

Product decision on lifecycle endpoints or evidence supporting an optional additional active slot without violating clarity.
